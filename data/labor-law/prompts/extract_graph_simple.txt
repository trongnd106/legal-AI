-Goal-
Extract semantic entities and relationships from Vietnamese labor law text.

-Entity Types-
{entity_types}

-Classification Rules (MUST follow)-
HanhVi         = Legal actions/violations: not paying salary, not signing contract, not registering insurance
CheTai         = Administrative penalties: fines, warnings per Decree 12/2022
XuLyKyLuat     = Internal disciplinary measures: termination, reprimand, salary delay
CoQuan         = State agencies: Ministry of Labor, Inspection, Government, Court
ChuThe         = Parties in labor relationship: worker, employer, trainee, organization
HopDongLaoDong = Labor contract: employment agreement, terms, conditions
TienLuong      = Salary: salary level, salary structure, minimum wage, coefficient
TraLuong       = Salary payment: payment schedule, payment method, late payment
ThoiGioLamViec = Working time: working hours, overtime, schedule
NghiPhep       = Leave: annual leave, sick leave, maternity leave, unpaid leave
CheDoBaoHiem   = Insurance: social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance
TroCapThoiViec = Termination benefits: severance, compensation for early termination
AnToanVeSinhLaoDong = Occupational health and safety: accident prevention, safety measures

-Output Format-
For each entity found, output one line:
("entity"<|>entity_name<|>entity_type<|>entity_description)

For each relationship found, output one line:
("relationship"<|>source_entity<|>target_entity<|>relationship_description<|>strength_1_to_10)

Use ## as delimiter between records.

-Important Instructions-
1. Do NOT extract: VanBan, Chuong, Dieu, Khoan, Diem (already in database)
2. Extract only entities that match {entity_types}
3. entity_name: concise name, title case
4. entity_type: MUST be one of {entity_types}
5. entity_description: brief Vietnamese description, clear legal meaning
6. relationship_strength: integer 1-10
7. Return complete output as plain text with delimiters, NO JSON

-Example-
("entity"<|>Thử việc<|>HanhVi<|>Giai đoạn thử việc của người lao động mới)
##
("entity"<|>Ba tháng<|>ThoiGioLamViec<|>Thời gian tối đa thử việc)
##
("relationship"<|>Thử việc<|>Ba tháng<|>Có thời hạn tối đa là<|>9)
